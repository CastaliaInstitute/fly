A NOTE ON THE SCIENCE

A model to question,
not a fly in a box.

This prototype uses a synthetic, stylized circuit inspired by mushroom-body learning. It does not load FlyWire connections, reproduce a published neuron-by-neuron model, or establish that a fly’s cognition works this way.

The adult fly connectome gives us wiring and annotations. Learning additionally depends on synaptic plasticity, neuromodulation, timing, and biological state—details this toy model supplies as assumptions.
